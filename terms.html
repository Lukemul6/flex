Community rules

  1. Be a good sport. Trash talk is part of a battle, abuse isn't. No harassment, bullying, threats or hate towards anyone.
  2. Keep names and photos clean. No sexual, violent, hateful or offensive names, handles, photos or competition names. Offensive words are blocked automatically.
  3. Only real reps. Don't cheat the counter or try to fake sets. Leaderboards and battles only count verified reps. Sets that look impossible are checked and can be removed, and cheaters can be hidden from leaderboards and competitions.
  4. Report and block. Tap ••• on anyone to report or block them. Reports go straight to us and are reviewed within 24 hours.
  5. Zero tolerance. We remove content that breaks these rules and remove accounts that do it, without warning if needed.

Flex Pro

Flex is free to use. Flex Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) that unlocks extra features such as training plans, ghost races, sending challenges, timed sets, more battle modes, full stats, progress and records, creating competitions, extra app icons and exporting your workouts. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically at the same price for the same period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; you can manage or cancel it in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and may vary by country.
